Weekly Rankings

QB, RB , WR, TE and Flex rankings based on your league settings and season averages

The two sliders always add up to 100%. Moving one moves the other.

Score. A blend of Sleeper's projected points for the week you pick and his average: points per game this season, or over his last 3 games if you choose that. The default is 75% projection and 25% average; use the sliders to change the mix (they always add up to 100%) and Reset to go back. Both use your league's own scoring. Games he didn't play (byes, injuries) don't count against his average. A player with no games yet (a rookie, or someone back from a long injury) uses his projection for the whole score.
Changing weeks. Pick any week of the season. For a past week, form only counts games before that week, so the rankings are what they would have said at the time, and the Result column shows what each player actually scored and where he finished at his position. Future weeks use every game played so far.
Owners are today's rosters. Players Sleeper projects for 0 points (bye or out) are left off the list, except when you show one team, where they're listed at the bottom so you can see who's missing. FLEX ranks running backs, receivers and tight ends together.
Sorting. Tap any column header to sort by it, and tap again to flip the order. Tap # to go back to the rankings.
