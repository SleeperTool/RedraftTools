Defense Streamer

A better way to stream Fantasy Defenses. How bad is the offense ? How good is the defense ? Do you have custom defensive scoring? This ranks every NFL defense for every week through Week 18 based on your preferences. Pick up to three weeks to compare.

Find it in the Sleeper link for your league.

36%

How many defense points the opposing offense usually hands out.

24%

How many points this defense scores compared with an average one.

40%

Sleeper's own projected defense stats, scored with your league's settings.

The three always add to 100%.
How the score works. Each defense's score is the points we expect it to score in your league that week. It mixes the three weights above: how generous the opponent's offense has been, how good the defense itself is, and Sleeper's projection.
The first two come from every completed game this season, scored with your league's own defense settings. The model estimates defense quality and offense generosity together, so a defense is not credited for facing weak offenses. Early in the season the estimates are pulled toward average.
It does not see Vegas lines, the opponent's quarterback news or the weather. Use it to build a shortlist, then check those for your top picks.
