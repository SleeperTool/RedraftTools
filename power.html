Score = 50% record, 30% points for, 20% roster strength, each as a z-score scaled to 0–100 (50 = league average). Made with Sleeper Tools.

How the score works. Each team gets three numbers: win percentage, total points scored, and roster strength. Each one is turned into a z-score (how far above or below the league average, in standard deviations). The z-scores are weighted 50% record, 30% points and 20% roster, then scaled to a 0–100 score where 50 is average: score = (weighted z + 2.5) ÷ 5 × 100.
Roster strength uses the same player values as the Trade Calculator: rest-of-season value from what each player has done and is projected to do (half each), with a small bye penalty, counting only what he adds over the best free agents. A team's roster value is the total value of its best legal starting lineup, plus 15% of the value of its top six bench players for depth. Stars count extra, because values follow the same curve as the trade calculator.
