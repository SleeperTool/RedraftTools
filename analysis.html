League Analysis

Median Scoring Standings and All Play Standings

Median Scoring Standings

Every week each team plays its real opponent and also the league median. Score above the median is a second win, below it is a second loss. The arrow shows the change from the real head-to-head standings.

All Play Standings

Every week each team plays every other team. Beat 9 of 11 teams and that week counts 9-2. It shows who would lead with no schedule luck. Luck is real wins minus expected wins (all-play win rate times games played).

Which weeks count. Finished regular-season weeks only. Playoff weeks and the week in progress are left out. Ties with the median (exactly the median score) count as a tie.
Seasons. Past seasons come from Sleeper's link between each season's league and the one before it. If your league was recreated instead of renewed in Sleeper, older seasons won't appear. All time adds up every season by manager, so a manager keeps their history even if their team name or roster slot changed. The name shown is their latest team name.
Sorting. Tap any column header to sort that table, and tap again to flip the order. Tap # to go back to the standings.
