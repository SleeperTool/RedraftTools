Type in any standings, or load them from Sleeper. The odds redraw every time you change something. For the full injury-aware model, use the Playoff Odds page.

Teams are seeded by record first (ties broken by points scored). Any spots set to points go to the highest scorers who missed on record. A common setup is 7 spots with 1 by points.

How it works. Each team's scoring level starts from its points per game so far, pulled toward the league average when few games have been played. Every simulated season draws a level for each team, plays out the rest of the schedule with random weekly scores, and applies your seeding rules. The odds are the share of simulated seasons where a team gets in.
Loaded from Sleeper, it uses your real remaining schedule. Typed in by hand, it pairs teams at random for the games they have left. It does not know about injuries, byes or roster moves, so treat it as a standings-based estimate.
